### Exercises
1. Personal RCM origin story
  - 1 person: How did you become/get interested in/find out about RCM as a role? (3-5 min)
  - Everyone else takes notes
  - Reflect on how you received the story (5 min)
    - What details stood out to you?
    - Did you notice specific inflection points?
    - How do your notes differ from eachother?
2. Collaborative origin story (10 min)
  - Redraft this origin story together. It should be/do:
    - Concise, easy to remember, and both answer & provoke questions
    - Articulate our values, shape identity, tradition and expectations
    - Provide an entrance into *this* community of RCMs (‘generous exclusion’)
  - What does it mean for *this* community if we emphasise different parts of our origins?
  - Who are the audiences for this story?
