**Our monthly meet-ups are a place for all those who support, endorse or are interested in communities for researchers.**

**This month’s theme: RCM Narratives**

The session uses narratives, especially origin stories, to help RCMs describe what their job is to people who don't know it. These narratives do two things: (1) giving a community a shared identity internally (2) making RCM work legible to outsiders.

**Speaker**

- **\[Michael Donnay\](https://www.software.ac.uk/our-people/michael-donnay)**, Community Manager, Institute for Research Software

**Host**

- **\[Danny Garside\](https://www.rcmcooperative.com/team/Danny-Garside/),**Trainer Community Manager, Digital Research Academy

Our Monthly Meetups aim to build connections between people who have **formal or informal interests in maintaining research communities**, helping us learn and develop together, and leaving us all better equipped to build sustainable research communities.

Monthly meet-ups will feature talks and facilitated discussions, with both invited speakers and call hosts receiving an honoraria (Funded by CHARTED).
